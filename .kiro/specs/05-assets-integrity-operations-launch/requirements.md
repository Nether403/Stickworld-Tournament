## Requirements

### R1 — Build-time asset pipeline

1. Art MAY be generated with Gemini and voice with Deepgram, **offline and
   operator-invoked only**, writing gitignored files under
   `assets/generated/`.
2. **No runtime calls to Gemini, Deepgram, or OpenRouter in any shipped
   gameplay path.** CI SHALL fail if client or worker bundles contain those
   hostnames, and a Playwright ranked (or practice, if ranked needs auth)
   run SHALL assert zero requests to those hosts.
3. OpenRouter SHALL remain development-tooling only. It SHALL NOT appear in
   `apps/`, `packages/`, or `games/` runtime imports.
4. Collision geometry SHALL remain the hand-authored TypeScript in
   `games/*/src/simulation/**`. Asset files SHALL NOT define colliders.
5. Shipped binaries that the web app serves SHALL be committed under
   `apps/web/public/assets/` (ADR-0007 decision 3). `pnpm assets:build` SHALL
   NOT be a Railway or CI build step.
6. `docs/assets/ledger.md` SHALL list every shipped file with SHA-256,
   class (`human` | `generated` | `generated-then-human-edited`), and source
   path. CI SHALL fail on an unlisted file in `apps/web/public/assets/` or a
   listed file that is missing.
7. Brand marks (logo, mascot, wordmark) SHALL be `human` or
   `generated-then-human-edited`, living first in `sources/brand/`.
8. Changing shipped art or audio SHALL NOT regenerate determinism goldens.
   If a visual change would require a sim change, stop; that is a new
   `game_version`, not Spec 5 cosmetics.
9. Announcer voice SHALL NOT block launch (ADR-0007 decision 4).

### R2 — The PvP seam (preserve the option, build nothing)

1. `@stickworld/input` SHALL export `InputSource` with
   `inputsForTick(tick: number): readonly QuantisedInput[]`.
2. `LocalInputSource` and `ReplayInputSource` SHALL be real implementations.
   `ScriptedInputSource` SHALL stand in for a future network source in tests.
   **`NetworkInputSource` SHALL NOT be implemented.**
3. A test SHALL drive one `StickworldGame` (Test Chamber is enough) from
   all three sources with identical `score` and `stateHash`.
4. `playReplay` SHALL apply ticks through `InputSource`. GameHost's per-tick
   apply path SHALL too. Hookline freeze hash SHALL remain `9c52d8f426f31ee1`;
   Pickaxe `6b03896db5837763`.
5. `docs/adr/0008-pvp-seam.md` SHALL record what an authoritative-room model
   would add (rooms, matchmaking, per-tick broadcast, reconciliation) and
   what it would not change (sim-core, `SWR1`, scoring, schema, worker).
6. **No rooms, matchmaking, netcode, or rollback net SHALL be built.**

### R3 — Integrity, abuse, and compliance

1. An adversarial suite SHALL cover, as automated tests:
   malformed, truncated, oversized, bad-CRC, and unknown-version replays via
   `POST /v1/attempts/:id/finish`; duplicate nonce / consumed attempt;
   expired attempt; finish after ranked navigation-away (attempt left
   `issued`/`active` then expiry); clock skew (client `Date` vs server
   `expiresAt` — server clock wins); rate-limit trip and a second identity
   not sharing the first's user bucket.
2. Refresh mid-ranked SHALL abandon (competitive-spec §7). GameHost `stop`
   without finish SHALL NOT POST finish (already true). A test SHALL show
   the attempt is not `verified`.
3. Ranked background-tab SHALL NOT pause (already true). A host unit test
   SHALL remain; no extra thinking time on the verifier.
4. Handles remain UGC. `POST /v1/reports` SHALL accept reports from any
   visitor (no session), rate-limited by IP, targeting a handle or profile
   id, with a reason code.
5. A moderation queue SHALL list open reports. Moderators
   (`profiles.role = 'moderator'`) SHALL dismiss, force-release a handle,
   suspend, or unsuspend. Every action SHALL write `audit_events` and a
   `moderation_actions` row.
6. The actioned user SHALL see a statement of reasons at
   `GET /v1/me/notices` naming `reason_code`, human text, and that they may
   reply by emailing the operator address published on `/legal` (no in-app
   mail vendor).
7. `GET /v1/me/export` SHALL return JSON of the caller's profile, attempts,
   runs (replay as base64), verified results, reports they filed, and audit
   rows where they are `actor`. `POST /v1/me/delete` SHALL anonymise per
   ADR-0007 decision 11. Leaderboard snapshots SHALL still rebuild; the
   row displays `retired`.
8. `normalizeHandle` SHALL reject handles that collide with the anonymised
   `d-[0-9a-f]{12}` pattern.
9. Public responses SHALL keep existing non-leaky mappings
   (`packages/platform/src/reason-codes.ts`).
10. `apps/web` SHALL send security headers: `X-Content-Type-Options:
    nosniff`, `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`,
    `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, `Permissions-Policy` disabling camera/mic/geo,
    and a `Content-Security-Policy` whose `connect-src` includes `'self'`
    and the Neon Auth origin and **does not** include Gemini, Deepgram, or
    OpenRouter hosts. Phaser/Rapier `wasm-unsafe-eval` MAY be listed if
    required; document the exception in `docs/ops/security-headers.md`.
11. Age policy: 13+ self-declared (ADR-0007 decision 9). Copy on `/legal`
    and the email signup form.

### R4 — Cross-browser, device, and performance QA

1. Web e2e SHALL run Playwright projects: Desktop Chrome, Desktop Firefox,
   Desktop WebKit, plus existing `iPhone 12` (mobile WebKit viewport) and
   `Pixel 5` (mobile Chromium viewport).
2. Per shipping game, CI SHALL continue `score:browser` (Chromium/Firefox/
   WebKit simulation). That remains the physics matrix. Render frame-time
   and RSS memory are **demo gates**, not CI.
3. CI SHALL fail if:
   - a play page fetches another game's `./client` chunk (already partly
     covered; extend to all ten slugs),
   - `scripts/check-play-bundle.mjs` exceeds the Spec 3 ceiling,
   - any sample replay exceeds its class cap (design §6),
   - `scripts/check-no-runtime-ai.mjs` finds a banned host.
4. Real mid-range Android and iPhone passes SHALL be demo gates recorded in
   `docs/ops/device-qa.md` for the two heaviest games (Demolition Dive,
   Balance Bike Blitz) plus Hookline.
5. Per-game load-time and validation-duration numbers SHALL be written to
   `docs/budgets/spec5-qa.md` when measured. Missing real-device numbers
   SHALL block Task 6 public launch, not Task 4 merge, provided CI matrix
   is green.

### R5 — Operations on Railway and Neon

1. One Railway project, three processes: `web` (`pnpm --filter
   @stickworld/web start`), `worker` (`pnpm --filter @stickworld/worker
   start`), cron (`pnpm --filter @stickworld/worker cron <job>` for
   `recompute-rankings`, `rotate-daily`, `close-season`). Worker SHALL NOT
   be publicly routed.
2. Release command for `web` SHALL run `pnpm db:migrate` against
   `DATABASE_URL_UNPOOLED` before `start`. Worker SHALL refuse to start if
   migrations are pending (check Drizzle journal vs files).
3. `@stickworld/telemetry` SHALL write one JSON object per `emit` to stdout
   when `STICKWORLD_TELEMETRY=1` (Railway web, worker, cron). Tags SHALL
   include `gameId`, `gameVersion`, `seasonId`, `browserFamily`,
   `deviceClass` (`desktop | mobile | unknown`), `mode`. New event names:
   `verify.ok`, `verify.reject`, `verify.duration_ms`, `attempt.issue`,
   `attempt.finish`. Games SHALL still run if env is unset (no-op).
4. `GET /v1/health` SHALL return 200 when the pooled DB answers `select 1`.
   Railway healthcheck uses it. Do not expose internals.
5. `docs/ops/rollback.md` SHALL describe Railway rollback of `web` and
   `worker` independently, and that schema roll-forward is preferred
   (Drizzle down-migrate is not the production path; see
   `packages/db/README.md`).
6. `docs/ops/pitr-restore.md` SHALL describe Neon PITR into a new branch,
   `pnpm db:migrate` (no-op if already applied), and a ranking rebuild from
   `verified_results` compared to the frozen snapshot. This is a **demo
   gate**.
7. Neon production autosuspend stays **disabled** (ADR-0004). Record it
   again in `docs/ops/neon.md` so Spec 5 does not relitigate it.
8. No CDN. No Sentry. No Cloudflare in this spec (ADR-0007).
9. Production Railway **web** and **worker** env SHALL NOT contain
   `GEMINI_API_KEY`, `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`, or `OPENROUTER_API_KEY`.

### R6 — Internal tournament

1. Season slug `internal-0`, `entry_policy = 'invite'`, staff accounts only,
   on the production Railway/Neon project (or a dedicated production-shaped
   Railway environment that is not public DNS). Synthetic users SHALL NOT
   be written to that database.
2. The edge-case list SHALL be exercised; each item SHALL have an automated
   test (Task 3) even if the human pass also checks it:
   extreme scores (envelope reject), ties, zero scores, disconnect /
   refresh mid-run, duplicate submission, long and malformed replays,
   expired attempts, background tab (ranked hitch, not pause), mobile
   viewport touch, finish after a killed connection (idempotent retry),
   worker backlog (jobs drain), season `close-season` while an attempt is
   still unexpired (finish rejected with `SEASON_INACTIVE` or the run is
   verified then the snapshot freezes — pick one in design §8 and test it).
3. Close `internal-0` with the existing cron. Snapshot frozen.

### R7 — Closed beta

1. Season slug `beta-0`, `entry_policy = 'invite'`, 24 emails in
   `ranked_invites`, 14 days.
2. Measure (SQL notes in `docs/ops/metrics.md`): attempts per player, game
   popularity, score distributions, mismatch rate, abandonment
   (`issued` never finished), errors by `game_id`.
3. Look for games where a small input exploit yields outlier scores. Any
   fix is a **versioned** `game_version`, never a silent edit of
   `verified_results`.
4. Championship will be **provisional** (24 < 50). That is expected; the
   provisional badge SHALL be visible.

### R8 — Version freeze and launch

1. Public season slug `season-1`, `entry_policy = 'open'`, 28 days.
2. Before opening it, freeze: Rapier pin, detmath, replay format,
   per-game `game_version` / `simulation_version` / `scoring_version`,
   championship formula as amended, attempt TTL, rate limits, physics
   budgets in manifests.
3. Publish `docs/rulebook.md` (player-facing excerpt of
   `docs/competitive-spec.md` v2, including nine-game championship).
   Publish `docs/known-issues.md`.
4. Competitive-spec §11 SHALL be amended to nine championship games
   (ADR-0007 decision 6) as part of this freeze. That is a
   `rules_version` bump.
5. Working titles remain until the legal track clears them. Public launch
   SHALL NOT proceed if counsel has not reviewed branding and silhouettes
   (`docs/legal/brand-and-ip-clearance.md`). Internal and beta MAY proceed
   on working titles.
6. Only critical integrity fixes SHALL ship after freeze, each as a new
   `game_version` or platform patch that cannot change a score.

## Launch quality bar

**Competitive integrity**
- [ ] Client cannot submit an authoritative score
- [ ] Ranked runs require server-issued attempt ids and server-controlled seeds
- [ ] **Every** ranked run is verified server-side by re-simulation
- [ ] Duplicate submissions rejected
- [ ] Game, simulation, scoring versions and Rapier build all pinned per season
- [ ] Tie rules and the championship formula published (nine fixed-course games)

**Game stability**
- [ ] All ten games pass deterministic replay tests across all four runtimes
- [ ] CI browser matrix green (R4.1)
- [ ] Real-device demo recorded for the two heaviest games plus Hookline
- [ ] No known leaderboard-breaking exploit (or it is on known-issues as
      practice-only until versioned)

**Platform**
- [ ] Accounts reliable; personal bests persist correctly
- [ ] Per-game and championship rankings accurate; Pogo off championship
- [ ] Every leaderboard rebuildable from `verified_results`
- [ ] Replay storage functioning; PITR restore demo done

**Operations**
- [ ] Structured telemetry on web, worker, cron
- [ ] `/v1/health` used as the Railway probe
- [ ] Rollback documented
- [ ] GDPR export/delete and handle moderation live

## Definition of done

- [ ] Ledger complete; `check-no-runtime-ai` green; ranked/practice network
      test shows zero AI hosts
- [ ] Brand marks in `sources/brand/` classified `human` or
      `generated-then-human-edited`
- [ ] `InputSource` three-source test green; ADR-0008 written; no netcode
- [ ] Adversarial suite green; a report flows through the queue with an
      audit trail and a statement of reasons
- [ ] GDPR export and deletion working; standings still rebuild
- [ ] CI device/browser matrix green against the budgets in design §6
- [ ] Rollback runbook exists; healthcheck green on `web`
- [ ] PITR restore demo recorded
- [ ] `internal-0` closed with a frozen snapshot
- [ ] `beta-0` metrics reviewed; `season-1` freeze applied; rulebook published
