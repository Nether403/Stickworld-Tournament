# Pogo Tower — inspiration ledger

**Abstract lesson taken**

Auto-bounce plus lean to land on shrinking ledges is an unprotectable timing
problem. A weekly seed is our competitive hook.

**Explicitly excluded from copying**

Third-party tower/climb skins, named peak campaigns, Doodle-style enemy faces,
and copy-pasted platform sequences from commercial pogo games. No third-party
title in code or assets.

**Our independent creative pillars**

- Generator: exactly 16 ledges, y = 2 + i×1.8, x and movers from PRNG draws in
  a published order.
- Weekly-seed ranked format; championship ignores these rows.
- Capsule climber; movers are kinematic (Wave B primitive).
