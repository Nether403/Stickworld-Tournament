# Launch Lab — inspiration ledger

**Abstract lesson taken**

Choosing an angle and a power, then committing a projectile to gravity, is an
unprotectable aiming problem. Three counted launches average luck.

**Explicitly excluded from copying**

Third-party carnival booth silhouettes, clown/target art, named amusement
titles, upgrade shops, and any "human cannonball" costume sets. No third-party
title in code, strings, fixtures, or asset names.

**Our independent creative pillars**

- Four authored rings plus a landing deck with a settle rule (20 ticks, |v|<0.8).
- Locked capsule, not a ragdoll. Sum of three sub-scores inside one replay.
- Shared `@stickworld/ui` tokens and Hookline capsule proportions.
