# Balance Bike Blitz — inspiration ledger

**Abstract lesson taken**

Throttle, brake, and lean on two wheels over ramps is an unprotectable vehicle
problem.

**Explicitly excluded from copying**

Trials-game course palettes, branded bikes, rider costumes, ghost-bike UX from
commercial titles, and collapsing-structure set pieces copied from a particular
campaign. No third-party title in code or assets.

**Our independent creative pillars**

- Kit wheel assembly with published masses and rest lengths.
- Static ramps (no movers — Pogo owns movers). Checkpoints at 16/32/48/64.

**Asset provenance**

v1: Phaser primitives. Numbers in `docs/games/balance-bike-blitz.md`.
