# Demolition Dive — inspiration ledger

**Abstract lesson taken**

Spending a body's momentum to break a stack is an unprotectable demolition
problem. Caps and despawn rules are our competitive expression.

**Explicitly excluded from copying**

Third-party city skylines, branded wrecking balls, "dismount" scoring of joint
snaps, blood FX, and any commercial demolition-title building kits. No
third-party title in code or assets.

**Our independent creative pillars**

- Twelve authored cuboids, three storeys, published values, chain depth cap 3.
- Fracture does not spawn shards (no new bodies). Despawn parks the same
  handle. Three dives, sum, one replay.
- Ships last; cut is allowed (nine championship columns).
