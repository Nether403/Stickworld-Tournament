# Rooftop Relay — inspiration ledger

**Abstract lesson taken**

Carrying speed across gaps is an unprotectable parkour problem. Jump and slide
are the verbs.

**Explicitly excluded from copying**

Third-party city skylines, parkour-game vault animations, named districts,
collectible-bit economies, and ragdoll-stumble as a sim switch. Stumble is
cosmetic only. No third-party title in code or assets.

**Our independent creative pillars**

- Kinematic controller, seven authored roofs, two lintels, six checkpoint
  planes, finish x≥72.
- Flow streak on checkpoints; fail resets it, stumble does not.

**Asset provenance**

v1: Phaser primitives. Numbers in `docs/games/rooftop-relay.md`.
